# Energy Aftercare Website

A redesigned public marketing website prototype for Energy Aftercare — an independent UK provider of aftercare and technical support for solar PV, battery storage and EV charging systems.

**Audience:** homeowners, businesses and renewable installers in the UK.

**Intent:** a design and layout upgrade of www.energyaftercare.co.uk. All wording is copied exactly from the live site and kept in `src/content/` so it can be edited easily. Forms, log in, chat and bookings are front-end only for now. Real photography is dropped into `src/assets/images/<slot>.*` and appears automatically.
