# MH Entertainment

**MH Entertainment** is a premium music industry website for a record label / Maison de Disque. The site presents a sophisticated black-and-white editorial aesthetic aimed at attracting professional artists, producers, and music industry professionals.

## Who it's for

- **Artists & Producers** submitting music projects, radio singles, and music videos
- **Music industry professionals** seeking partnerships, licensing, and collaboration opportunities
- **MH Entertainment's internal team** reviewing submissions via the database

## What it does

- **Landing page** — Cinematic hero, about section, and submission hub with four premium cards
- **Submit My Project** — Multi-step form for general music project submissions
- **Radio Single Submission** — Dedicated form for radio promotion consideration
- **TV Music Video Submission** — Form for television and media placement
- **Other Opportunities** — Catch-all for licensing, partnerships, press, and collaborations

All submissions are stored in the Submissions database table for the team to review and manage with status tracking (New → Under Review → Accepted / Not Selected).
